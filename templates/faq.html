FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does restaurant matching work?

A Restaurant Discovery Agent applies local filters — cuisine, budget, diet, occasion, and location — to your dataset, then AI reasons over the strongest candidates using a transparent, weighted scoring model rather than arbitrary numbers.

How does the AI analyze menus?

A Menu Analysis Agent reads each restaurant's actual menu records to surface best-value dishes, dietary-friendly options, popular and premium items, and dishes suited to sharing — grounded in real dataset fields, not guesses.

Can I specify dietary requirements?

Yes. You can select common dietary needs or enter a custom restriction. A Dietary Compatibility Agent assesses menu items and labels each as Likely Compatible, Needs Verification, Not Suitable, or Unknown. This assessment is informational only — always confirm ingredients and preparation directly with the restaurant for serious allergies.

Can I compare restaurants?

Yes, up to three restaurants at once. A Comparison Agent scores each on cuisine, budget, dietary fit, atmosphere, occasion, value, and overall match, and provides a clear written verdict explaining why one option wins.

Can I create a dining plan?

Yes — the Dining Planner builds a complete plan with a recommended order across courses, an estimated total cost, and an explanation of why the plan fits your budget, party size, and occasion.

Does the platform provide live restaurant availability?

No. Availability is not connected to any real booking provider. Where a booking action might otherwise imply real-time availability, we show "Availability not connected" and recommend checking with the restaurant directly.

How is my data stored?

There is no login. Saved restaurants, recently viewed history, and session preferences live in your browser's local storage on your device. Feedback and contact submissions are stored server-side for product improvement, without creating a personal account.
