About

A dining decision platform, not another directory.

AI Restaurant Intelligence helps you answer one question well: where should I eat, and why is this the best option for me? Instead of a long list of restaurants, you get a reasoned recommendation.

What the platform does

We combine a curated restaurant dataset with a set of specialized AI agents that reason about your preferences — cuisine, budget, diet, occasion, and atmosphere — to surface restaurants that actually fit, and explain exactly why.

How restaurant intelligence works

A Restaurant Discovery Agent first narrows down candidates using local filtering — cuisine, location, price, diet, and occasion — before any AI reasoning happens. A Restaurant Intelligence Agent then evaluates the remaining candidates for atmosphere, budget fit, and occasion suitability, and produces a transparent match score alongside its reasoning.

How AI agents analyze dining options

Each agent has a narrow responsibility: discovery, restaurant intelligence, menu analysis, dietary compatibility, comparison, and dining plan construction. An orchestrator calls only the agents needed for a given request, so we never send your entire query to every agent unnecessarily.

How menu intelligence works

A Menu Analysis Agent reads each restaurant's actual menu data to identify best-value dishes, dietary-friendly options, popular items, and dishes that pair well together — so you know what to order before you arrive.

How personalization works

Your preferences are remembered for the current session and, where you choose to save something, stored in your browser's local storage. We don't use accounts or cloud-based persistent memory — nothing is stored on a server tied to your identity.

A note on data: Restaurant listings, menus, and reviews in this product are realistic fictional sample data built for demonstration purposes. They are not verified real-world facts, and availability is not connected to any live booking system.
